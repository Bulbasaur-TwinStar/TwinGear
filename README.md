# ⚔️ TwinGear v2.2 — The Ultimate Gear Audit, Stat Engine, Group & Raid Scanner & Account Roster for TwinStar (MoP 5.4.8)

**TwinGear** is a lightweight, zero-bloat, all-in-one character audit, live stat priority advisor, universal group/raid readiness scanner (5 / 10 / 25 / 40 players), and account-wide alt manager engineered specifically for **TwinStar WoW — Mists of Pandaria 5.4.8** (**Helios** & **Persés**).

* **Author:** Bulbasaur of TwinStar
* **Client Support:** 100% compatible across **all WoW 5.4.8 client languages** (EN, FR, DE, ES, PT, RU) and alongside **DragonUI, ElvUI, or default Blizzard UI**.

---

## 🔥 Why Every TwinStar Player Needs TwinGear

### 1. 📊 Full 11-Class & 34-Spec Stat Priority Engine
No more guessing stat weights or missing caps before a raid:
* **Automatic Spec Stat Sorting:** Automatically arranges your stats in exact **MoP 5.4.8 stat priority order** for every single class and specialization (**all 34 specs supported**).
* **Live Hit & Expertise Cap Advisor:**
  * Tracks Caster Hit Cap (**15.00%**) with exact rating surplus/deficit calculation (tells you precisely how much Hit rating to reforge away or add).
  * Tracks Melee & Ranged Hit (**7.50%**) and Expertise (**7.50%** DPS / **15.00%** Tank Hard Cap).
  * Smart **Leveling Mode (`< 90`)** so leveling alts are never flagged with false red cap warnings.
* **Caster Haste Breakpoints:** Live tracking for **BP 1 (`5,273`)** and **BP 2 (`10,289`)** with exact rating needed for the next breakpoint.
* **Healer & PvP Precision:** Displays real **Combat Mana Regen (MP5)** for all healing specs and **PvP Power & PvP Resilience** whenever PvP gear is equipped.

### 2. 🛡️ Deep Equipment, Upgrade & Consumable Audit
* **True Equipped Item Level:** Instant, accurate equipped iLvl calculation for yourself, your inspected target, and group members (properly handles **Fury Warrior Titan's Grip 2×2H**, 2H weapons, shields, and off-hands).
* **Complete Gear & Gem/Enchant Verification:**
  * Detects **Legendary Cloak** (*Xing-Ho, Jina-Kang, Fen-Yu, Gong-Lu, Qian-Le, Qian-Ying*) + **Legendary Meta Gem**.
  * Tracks **Tier 14, Tier 15, and Tier 16** set bonuses (`2/5`, `4/5`) via language-independent Item IDs.
  * Audits **Item Upgrades** (`0/2` – `4/4`), **Reforged items**, **empty gem sockets**, **missing enchants**, **missing Living Steel Belt Buckles**, wrong armor specialization, and **profession perks** (*Engineering, Tailoring, Inscription, Leatherworking, Jewelcrafting, Enchanting*).
* **Full MoP Consumable Scanner:** Recognizes all MoP 5.4.8 **Flasks**, **Battle & Guardian Elixirs**, **Crystal of Insanity**, **Alchemist's Flask**, **Pandaren Noodle Carts**, **Banquets**, and `+250/+275/+300` stat food across every client language.

### 3. 👥 Universal Group & Raid Scanner (`Group Scan` – 5 / 10 / 25 / 40 Players)
Built for **5-man RDF & Challenge Modes**, **10/25-man Heroic, Normal & Flex raids**, and **40-man World Boss groups**:
* **Lag-Free Asynchronous Inspect Queue:** Safely scans **up to 40 players** across every group size with smooth mouse-wheel scrolling, cross-realm support (Helios & Persés), smart RDF bot detection, and zero FPS drops in combat.
* **Instant Raid Readiness Overview:** Displays Tank/Healer/DPS role breakdown, group average iLvl, missing buff tags (`[NoF/NoW]`), Legendary Cloak tags (`[Leg]`), and gear audit status (`OK` or missing gems/enchants).
* **Clean One-Click Raid Leader Chat Report:** Hover over **Report to Chat** for a full readiness summary, or click to announce **only missing Flasks & Food** directly to `/raid`, `/party`, or `/say` without spamming chat with gem/enchant details.
* **TwinGear Peer Sync & Auto-Update Alerts:** Automatically exchanges full primary profession skills (`600/600`) with other TwinGear users and notifies you in chat when a newer version is released on GitHub.### 4. 🌟 Account Roster, Currencies & Weekly Tracker (`Alts & MoP`)
Manage your entire TwinStar account from one clean screen:
* **Up to 50 Account Alts (Mouse-Wheel Scrollable):** Automatically saves every character you log into, sorted by iLvl with official **WoW Class Colors**, spec, level, Siege of Orgrimmar progression, and professions.
* **Daily Profession Cooldown Tracker:** Instantly see which alts have daily crafts ready (`[LW Hide]`, `[Liv.Steel]`, `[Cel.Cloth]`, `[Imp.Silk]`, `[Jard's]`, `[Bal.Tril]`, `[Light.Steel]`, `[JC Heart]`, `[Wisdom]`, `[Sha Cryst]`).
* **Primary & Secondary Professions:** Tracks both main professions and all 4 secondary professions (**Cooking, Fishing, Archaeology, First Aid**) with green `600/600` completion highlights.
* **Currencies & Weekly Checklist:**
  * Tracks **Timeless Coins**, **Valor Points + Weekly Cap (`X/1000`)**, **Justice Points**, **Warforged Seals**, and **Lesser Charms of Good Fortune** (with automatic `50+` weekly seal check).
  * Weekly kill status for all **6 MoP World Bosses** (**Sha of Anger, Galleon, Nalak, Oondasta, Celestials, Ordos**), weekly **Warforged Seal Bonus Roll** quest status, and **Ordos Cloak** access.

### 5. 🖥️ Clean UI Integration & Zero-Bloat Design
* **Character Frame (`C`) & Inspect Frame Buttons:** Sleek dark-gold **TwinGear** buttons integrated right into the top-right title bar of both frames.
* **Mouseover Unit Tooltip:** Hover over any player in the world to instantly see their **TwinGear iLvl** (color-coded by iLvl difference) and specialization.
* **Interactive 3D Character Preview & History:** Flicker-free rotatable 3D model, window scale controls (`-` / `+`), recent inspect history dropdown, draggable minimap icon, and native **Key Bindings** support.

## 🎮 Slash Commands & Controls

| Command / Control | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `/tg` or `/twingear` | Toggle the main TwinGear window |
| `/tgclearalts` | Reset the saved account character roster (`Alts & MoP`) |
| **Character (`C`) / Inspect Frame** | Click the dark-gold **TwinGear** button in the top-right header |
| **Key Bindings** | `Esc -> Key Bindings -> TwinGear v2.2` to bind a custom toggle key |

## 📥 Download & Installation

1. Click on **`TwinGear_v2.2_TwinStar_MoP.zip`** in the repository file list above, then click **Download raw file**.
2. Extract the `.zip` archive.
3. Copy the **`TwinGear`** folder directly into `World of Warcraft\Interface\AddOns\` (make sure there is no nested `TwinGear\TwinGear\` folder).
4. Restart the game client (or type `/reload` if already in-game).
